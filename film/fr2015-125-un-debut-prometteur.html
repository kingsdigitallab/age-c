Synopsis (original)

Martin, désabusé pour avoir trop aimé et trop vécu, retourne chez son père, un horticulteur romantique en fin de course. Il y retrouve Gabriel, son jeune frère de 16 ans, exalté et idéaliste, qu’il va tenter de dégoûter de l’amour, sans relâche. Mais c’est sans compter sur Mathilde, jeune femme flamboyante et joueuse, qui va bousculer tous leurs repères...

Synopsis

Martin, disillusioned by having loved and lived too much, returns to see his father, a romantic horticulturist reaching the end of his life. Once back home, he encounters Gabriel, his brother who is sixteen years his junior and a total, excitable idealist. Martin then tries to constantly put his younger sibling off love. But he hasn't taken into account Mathilde, a young, flamboyant and playful woman, who make them both lose all their bearings.
