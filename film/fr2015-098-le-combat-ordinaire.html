Synopsis (original)

Le « combat ordinaire » c’est le combat de Marco, jeune trentenaire, un brin bourru, mais animé de bonnes intentions et qui, à partir de petites choses, de belles rencontres, d’instants précieux, souvent tendres, parfois troublants, va se reconstruire et vaincre ses vieux démons.

Synopsis

The "ordinary combat" is the fight thirty-year-old Marco wages on a daily basis. Slightly surly, but motivated by good intentions, small things, fine encounters, precious, often tender and sometimes troubling moments will enable him to pull himself together and to overcome his old demons.
