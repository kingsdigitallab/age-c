Synopsis (original)

Une association accueille des jeunes LGBT mis à la rue par leurs familles. Derrière l’apparente comédie, les excès, l’envie de s'affirmer, se cachent des vies brisées. Tous ont cette furieuse envie d’exister, de trouver leur place dans la société. Ici, ils ont six mois, pour trouver un travail, un logement et s’accepter comme ils sont. Une course contre la montre durant laquelle Noëlle (Valérie Lemercier) qui dirige l’association et Alex (Finnegan Oldfield), qui s’y retrouve contre son gré, sont également renvoyés à leurs propres failles et s’interrogent sur leurs motivations à aider les autres.

Synopsis

Navigating the delicate balance between who they really are and their thirst for acceptance and love, a diverse group of LGBT youths rescued from the street have only six months! A painfully short amount of time to find a job, a place to live and a way to self-acceptance. They can’t stay any longer, the waiting lists are just too long… Struggling to stay out of trouble, estranged from his family, Alex has committed yet another petty crime and is assigned by his parole officer, to serve the community by working at the LGBT shelter for a month. Initially very reluctant, Alex can’t help but get attached to the damaged yet exceptional and endearing youths he encounters at the shelter. All the shelter’s volunteers and social workers must themselves battle their own flaws and sometimes even question their motivations for helping others… Alex comes to terms with the brutal fact that they just can’t rescue everyone. But if all they can save is a single life, it’s all worth it…
