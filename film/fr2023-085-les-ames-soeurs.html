Synopsis (original)

David, lieutenant des forces françaises engagées au Mali, est grièvement blessé dans une explosion. Rapatrié en France, il souffre d’amnésie et commence une longue convalescence sous le regard dévoué de sa sœur Jeanne. Dans la maison familiale des Pyrénées, entre montagnes et lacs, Jeanne tente de raviver sa mémoire, mais David ne parait pas soucieux de se réconcilier avec celui qu’il était.

Synopsis

After an attack against his military convoy in Mali, French lieutenant David Faber is brought back home to be cured. Deeply burnt, he spends months in recovery, under the careful watch of his worried and devoted sister Jeanne. But David suffers from severe amnesia. She brings him to their family house in the Pyrénées, between mountains, lakes and forests, where, she hopes, he will recover his memory.
