Synopsis (original)

Maxime Belloc est un grand joueur de football, le meilleur buteur du championnat. Dans un geste de colère il se brise la jambe... Son agent le contraint de se mettre au «vert» le temps de sa convalescence chez son père à qui il ne parle plus depuis 15 ans. Là-bas, à sa grande surprise, Maxime va redécouvrir le sens des valeurs… et de la famille !

Synopsis

Maxime Belloc is a great football player, the best goal-kicker of the championship. Lashing out in a moment of anger, he breaks his leg... His agent obliges him to "take a rest", which leads him to convalesce at his father's place. Maxime hasn't spoken to his father for fifteen years. Back home, to his great surprise, Maxime rediscovers his values... and his family!
