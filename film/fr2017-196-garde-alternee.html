Synopsis (original)

Sandrine, mariée depuis quinze ans, deux enfants, découvre que son mari Jean a une relation extraconjugale. Passé le choc, elle décide de rencontrer sa rivale, Virginie, et lui propose un étrange marché : prendre Jean en garde alternée. Les deux femmes se mettent d’accord et imposent à leur homme ce nouveau mode de vie.

Synopsis

Sandrine, married for fifteen years and a mother of two children, discovers that her husband Jean is having an affair. Once she has got over the shock, she decides to meet her rival and offers her a strange deal: to have "shared custody" of Jean. Both women agree and impose this new way of life on their man.
