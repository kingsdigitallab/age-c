Synopsis (original)

À la mort de son frère jumeau, Willy, 50 ans, quitte pour la première fois ses parents pour s’installer dans le village voisin. “À Caudebec, j’irai. Un appartement, j’en aurai un. Des copains, j’en aurai. Et j’vous emmerde !”. Inadapté, Willy part trouver sa place dans un monde qu’il ne connaît pas.

Synopsis

When his twin brother dies, 50-year-old Willy leaves his parents' home for the first time and moves to a neighboring village. "To Caudebec I shall go. An apartment I will find. Buddies I will have. And to hell with you all!" Maladjusted, Willy sets out to find his place in a world that he doesn't know.
