Synopsis (original)

Au petit matin, Pierre quitte Paul. Au volant de son Alfa Roméo, il traverse la France, ses plaines, ses montagnes, sans destination précise. Pierre utilise Grindr, une application de son téléphone portable qui recense et localise pour lui les occasions de drague. Mais Paul y a recours aussi pour mieux le suivre. Au terme de quatre jours et quatre nuits de rencontres – sexuelles ou non – parviendront-ils à se retrouver ?

Synopsis

At the break of day, Pierre leaves Paul. Behind the wheel of his Alfa Romeo, he travels through France, its plains and its mountains, heading in no particular direction. Pierre uses Grindr, the gay social network app, in order to arrange hook ups along the way. But Paul also uses it, the better to keep track of him. After four days and nights of encounters – sexual and non-sexual – will they manage to find each other again?
