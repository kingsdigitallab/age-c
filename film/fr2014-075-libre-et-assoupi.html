Synopsis (original)

Sébastien n’a qu’une ambition dans la vie : ne rien faire. Son horizon, c’est son canapé. Sa vie il ne veut pas la vivre mais la contempler. Mais aujourd’hui, si tu ne fais rien... Tu n’es rien. Alors poussé par ses deux colocs, qui enchainent stages et petits boulots, la décidée Anna et le pas tout à fait décidé Bruno, Sébastien va devoir faire ... Un peu.

Synopsis

Sébastien has only one ambition in life: to do nothing. His couch is his horizon. He doesn't want to live his life, he just wants to contemplate it. But, these days, if you don't do anything, you amount to nothing. Encouraged by his two roommates, determined Anna and vacillating Bruno, who go from internship to odd job, Sébastien must do... A little of something.
