Synopsis (original)

Dugometražni dokumentarni film o fenomenu senjskih uskoka, legendarnih gusara i protuosmanskih vojnika iz 16. i 17. stoljeća.

Synopsis

Single mother fights for her son. Eva takes care of her 13-year-old son Felix all alone. This is a huge challenge for her, because Felix suffers from Asperger's Syndrome: He finds it difficult to interact socially, which causes conflicts, especially at primary school. This responsibility is too big to handle for her.
