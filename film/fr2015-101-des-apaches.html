Synopsis (original)

Lors de l’enterrement de sa mère, Samir croise le regard d’un inconnu, son père. Celui-ci l’entraîne dans une affaire familiale qui le plonge au coeur de la population kabyle de Belleville et de ses traditions. Une expérience qui le bouleverse et fait ressurgir son passé d’une étrange manière. Alors qu’il se confronte à ses choix, Samir va s'affranchir de son enfance et de son clan pour devenir un homme libre, un « Apache ».

Synopsis

During his mother's funeral, Samir exchanges glances with a man he doesn't know: his father. His father drags him into a family affair that plunges Samir into the heart of the Kabyle population, and its traditions, of Paris's Belleville neighborhood. It is an experience that unsettles him and makes Samir's past rise to the surface in a strange way. Confronted by his choices, Samir must extricate himself from his childhood and his clan to become a free man, an "Apache."
