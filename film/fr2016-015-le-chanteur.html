Synopsis (original)

À la mort de sa mère, Thomas décide de s'affranchir d'une famille qui l'étouffe et d'une province qui le sclérose. Son but depuis toujours : devenir chanteur comme dans les comédies musicales qui ont bercé son enfance. Avec sa guitare, sa rage de vaincre et ses illusions pour seuls bagages, il débarque à Paris. Là, la réalité des choses s'offre violemment à lui. Mais, à force de rencontres et d'expériences, il entreprend un double voyage initiatique : professionnel et également... personnel.

Synopsis

When his mother dies, Thomas decides to free himself from the family that suffocates him and from the dead-end province where he lives. His aim has always to be become a singer, like in the musicals that he grew up with. With his guitar, his desire to conquer, and his illusions as his only luggage, he arrives in Paris. There, he is confronted by stark reality. But, by dint of encounters and experiences, he undertakes a double journey of initiation, professional and also... personal.
