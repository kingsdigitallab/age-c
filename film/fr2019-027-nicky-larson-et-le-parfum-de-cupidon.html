Synopsis

After having saved a child in Babysitting 1, caused chaos in Brazil in Babysitting 2 and made plausible the unthinkable in Alibi.com, Philippe Lacheau is back to save the world, well, at least part of it! Based on the world-famous Japanese manga City Hunter, Ryo Saeba (Philippe Lacheau) is THE private detective you go to to resolve the most complex cases. His one weakness: women! In this action-packed comedy, Ryo Saeba drags us through one crazy adventure after another. His first mission: to get his hands on the Cupid’s Perfume, a perfume that makes whoever sprays it become irresistible...
