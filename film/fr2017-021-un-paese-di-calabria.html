Synopsis (original)

Comme beaucoup de villages du sud de l’Italie, Riace a longtemps subi un exode rural massif. Un jour, un bateau transportant deux cents kurdes échoue sur la plage. Spontanément, les habitants du village leur viennent en aide. Petit à petit, migrants et villageois vont réhabiliter les maisons abandonnées, relancer les commerces et assurer un avenir à l’école. C’est ainsi que chaque jour depuis 20 ans, le futur de Riace se réinvente.

Synopsis

Like so many other villages undergoing a rural exodus, Riace - a small village situated in the extreme south of Italy - is slowing dying... But in 1998, a boat transporting 300 Kurds washed up on its beach. The spontaneous reaction of Riace's inhabitants, who decided to welcome the migrants be renovating abandoned houses, would profoundly modify its future. Today Riace is considered a model village of welcome by the Citta Futura project. But now the elections loom, and the opposition (supported by the local mafia) wants to get rid of the left-wing mayor. Will the inhabitants be able to defend what they've so courageously rebuilt?
