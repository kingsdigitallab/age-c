C’est là où ils font du skate, s’amusent et se défoncent, à deux pas du monde confiné des arts qu’ils côtoient sans le connaître. Certains sont inséparables, liés par des vies de famille compliquées. Synopsis

In Paris, at the Trocadéro. Every day, Math, Marie, Pacman, JP, Guillaume and Toff meet up at The Dome - behind the Museum of Modern Art, opposite the Eiffel Tower - skateboarding, goofing off or getting stoned as they ignore the smart crowd of art lovers. Bound by complicated family ties, some of them are inseparable. They live for the moment. They're lured by easy money, the anonymity of the Internet, and "youth, sex, drugs & rock'n'roll" parties. Toff films everything and all the time. Legendary director and photographer Larry Clark ventures further than ever before with this harsh and poetic tale of self-destructive Parisian youth.
