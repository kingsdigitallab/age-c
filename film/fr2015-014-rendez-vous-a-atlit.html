Synopsis (original)

Israël, 1995, la paix est enfin tangible. Dans la petite ville d'Atlit, Cali retrouve ses deux soeurs, Darel et Asia, pour vendre la maison héritée de leurs parents. Entre complicités et fou-rires réapparaissent les doutes et les vieilles querelles, ainsi que d’étranges convives qui sèment un joyeux bordel. Le 4 novembre, alors que le processus de paix est anéanti, les trois soeurs refusent d’abandonner l’espoir.

Synopsis

It's 1995 in Israel, and the peace process is in full swing. In the small town of Atlit, Cali meets with her sisters, Darel and Asia, to sell their childhood home which they have inherited from their parents. Between moments of complicity and hysterical laughter, old doubts and tensions resurface, and the appearance of unexpected guests adds to the chaos and confusion. On November 4, any hope of peace is suddenly shattered, but the three sisters refuse to abandon hope.
