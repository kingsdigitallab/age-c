Synopsis (original)

Nora Sator, jeune trentenaire dynamique, commence sa carrière dans la haute finance. Quand elle apprend que son patron et sa femme ont fréquenté son père dans leur jeunesse, elle découvre qu’une mystérieuse rivalité les oppose encore. Ambitieuse, Nora gagne vite la confiance de ses supérieurs mais entretient des rapports compliqués avec son collègue Xavier, contrairement à sa soeur Maya qui succombe rapidement à ses charmes... Entre histoires de famille, de coeur et intrigues professionnelles, les destins s’entremêlent et les masques tombent.

Synopsis

Nora Sator, a dynamic thirty-year-old, begins her career in high finance. When she learns that her boss and his wife knew her father in their youth, she discovers that a mysterious rivalry still brings them into conflict. Ambitious, Nora quickly earns the trust of her superiors, but maintains a complicated relationship with her colleague Xavier, unlike with his sister Maya, who rapidly succumbs to her charms. Between family upheavals, affairs of the heart, and professional intrigues, destinies become entangled and masks drop.
