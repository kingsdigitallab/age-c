Synopsis (original)

Documental que recoge la historia de la Abogacía española desde mediados de la década de 1960 a 1978, con imágenes inéditas y testimonios de prestigiosos abogados y abogadas que levantaron la bandera del derecho, la justicia y la democracia.

Synopsis

A portrait of the collective memory of the layers who put aside ideological differences to fight for democracy before the Spanish Transition. Some of them were imprisoned for their practice, others killed as in the murders of Atocha 55.
