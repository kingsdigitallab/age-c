Synopsis (original)

Tereza ima 37 godina, živi u Splitu. Nakon nekoliko spontanih pobačaja saznaje kako postoji vjerojatnost da genetski nije kompatibilna sa svojim suprugom. Već duže osjeća pritisak okoline te započne preispitivati svoj rutinirani brak, što potakne niz događaja koji će joj promijeniti život, u duhu novostečene slobode i istine koja je samo njena.

Synopsis

A 60-year-old Berlin actress is mugged by a young man. She faces the 17-year-old thief again when she, as a teacher, is tasked to help him with a speech impediment. The two grow close during language lessons in her apartment and leave Germany to travel to France together.
