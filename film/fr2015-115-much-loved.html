Synopsis (original)

Marrakech aujourd'hui. Noha, Randa, Soukaina, Hlima et les autres vivent d’amours tarifés. Ce sont des putes, des objets de désir. Les chairs se montrent, les corps s’exhibent et s’excitent, l’argent circule aux rythmes des plaisirs et des humiliations subies. Mais joyeuses et complices, dignes et émancipées dans leur royaume de femmes, elles surmontent la violence d’une société marocaine qui les utilise tout en les condamnant.

Synopsis

Marrakech today. Noha, Randa, Soukaina, Hlima, and other women make a living from love for sale. They are prostitutes, objects of desire. Flesh that is shown, bodies that are exhibited and that excite, money that circulates to the rhythm of pleasure and humilations the women are subjected to. But, joyful and close, dignified and emancipated in their women's kingdom, they overcome the violence of Moroccan society, which they make use of, all the while condemning it.
