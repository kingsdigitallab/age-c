Synopsis (original)

Pauline, infirmière à domicile, entre Lens et Lille, s’occupe seule de ses deux enfants et de son père ancien métallurgiste. Dévouée et généreuse, tous ses patients l'aiment et comptent sur elle. Synopsis

Pauline, a home help nurse, working between Lens and Lille, raises her two children alone, while also looking after her father, a former steelworker. Devoted and generous, all her patients love and depend on her. Taking advantage of her popularity, the directors of an extremist political party suggest she become their candidat at the mayoral elections.
