Synopsis (original)

Knock, un ex-filou repenti devenu médecin diplômé, arrive dans le petit village de Saint-Maurice pour appliquer une « méthode » destinée à faire sa fortune : Il va convaincre la population que tout bien portant est un malade qui s’ignore. Et pour cela, trouver à chacun la maladie réelle ou imaginaire dont il souffre. Passé maître dans l’art de la séduction et de la manipulation, Knock est sur le point de parvenir à ses fins. Mais il est rattrapé par deux choses qu’il n’avait pas prévues : les sentiments du cœur et un sombre individu issu de son passé venu le faire chanter.

Synopsis

Knock, a retired crook-turned-qualified doctor, arrives in the small village of St-Maurice to implement a "method" he believes will make him rich: he will persuade the villagers that healthy people are simply people who don't realize they are ill. To do this, he needs to find an illness (be that real or imaginary) that afflicts each person. An expert in the art of seduction and manipulation, Knock comes close to achieving his goal. But his plans are rattled by two things he had not foreseen: his own romantic feelings and the appearance of a dubious character from his past who has come to blackmail him.
