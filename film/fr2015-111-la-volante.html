Synopsis (original)

Alors qu’il emmène sa femme à la maternité pour accoucher, Thomas percute et tue un jeune homme sur la route. Marie-France, la mère de ce dernier, ne parvient pas à se remettre du drame. Neuf ans plus tard, Marie-France devient la secrétaire de Thomas sans qu’il sache qui elle est. Peu à peu, elle s’immisce dangereusement dans sa vie et sa famille jusqu’à lui devenir indispensable.

Synopsis

While taking his wife to the hospital to give birth, Thomas knocks down and kills a young man on the road. Marie-France, the dead man's mother, has difficulty recovering from this dramatic event. Nine years later, Marie-France becomes Thomas' secretary, although he is oblivious to who she really is. Little by little she meddles dangerously with his life and his family until he can no longer do without her.
