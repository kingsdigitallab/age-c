Synopsis (original)

Años 40, una ciudad cualquiera. Un misterioso extranjero propone a cuatro inseparables amigos, que jugaban a ser una banda, un peligroso trabajo que llevará sus vidas por un camino sin retorno. Ricardo (Francisco Vera), un ambicioso limpiabotas, Amador (Octavi Pujades), un luchador clandestino de bodegas de barco, Marcelo (Domingo de Luis), un maquillador de cadáveres que iba para contable y Antonio (Borja Elgea), un mecánico cuyo mayor defecto eran las mujeres casadas, cambiarán para siempre sus vidas al aceptar la tentadora oferta de una vida mejor.

Synopsis

Spain, 1944, a country in turmoil, times are hard. Almost bankrupted by his late father's gambling debts, Ricardo, is forced to scrape a living as a shoe-shine boy, in order to care for his recently widowed sister and to keep them in their family home. Ricardo and his life-long buddies work together as a gang, extracting money from debtors for the town's rich and powerful. When the code of loyalty, that has bonded the four friends for so long, is tested to the limit, it takes them on a terrifying journey into an increasing spiral of violence.
