Synopsis (original)

Le concours d’entrée de la Fémis, Grande Ecole de cinéma, filmé pour la première fois. A travers ce documentaire, Claire Simon questionne le rapport entre les générations et le difficile parcours de sélection qu'organisent nos sociétés contemporaines.

Synopsis

With Place aux jeunes, Claire Simon will delve into the daily life of the Parisian cinema school, la Fémis, where a specific Republican ideal of excellence is practiced and entry into which can be summed up as follows: "everyone’s equal, but only the best get in…". Through the admission process, the hard work all year and the graduation exams, Place aux jeunes will establish a portrait of our relationship to excellence in the Art world and of one of the most prestigious art school in France. A place of culture where generations intermingle.
