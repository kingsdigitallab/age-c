Synopsis (original)

C’est dans l’enthousiasme que Sybille démarre l’écriture de son premier film. Actrice reconnue, elle va passer pour la première fois de l’autre côté de la caméra. Tout semble lui sourire. Ses productrices Brigitte et Ingrid sont deux personnages loufoques mais attachantes et Sybille se jette avec elles dans l’aventure, mettant de côté sa vie familiale. Mais, du choix improbable des actrices, aux réécritures successives du scénario, en passant par les refus des financiers, le rêve merveilleux va se transformer en cauchemar. Incorrigible optimiste, Sybille réalisera trop tard que ses productrices fantasques et totalement déjantées vont l’entraîner dans leur folie...

Synopsis

Sybille enthusiastically launches into writing the screenplay for her first film. A renowned actress, this is the first time she'll be stepping behind the camera to direct. Everything seems favorable to her success. Her producers Brigitte and Ingrid are two crazy but endearing characters and with them Sybille throws herself into this adventure, putting her family life on hold. But, from the improbable casting of the lead actresses to the successive rewrites of the screenplay, and not forgetting a lack of financiers willing to commit, the wonderful dream turns into a nightmare. An incorrigible optimist, Sybille will realize too late that her fanciful and totally insane producers are going to drag her into their madness.
