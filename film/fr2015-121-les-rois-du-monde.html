Synopsis (original)

À Casteljaloux, village du Sud-Ouest de la France, entre amitié, ivresse et plaisir du verbe, les hommes sont Les Rois du monde (Casteljaloux). Mais quand Jeannot sort de prison, il n’a qu’une seule idée en tête : reconquérir Chantal, l’amour de sa vie, qui s’est installée en son absence avec le boucher du village. La tragédie grecque prend alors des allures de western.

Synopsis

In Casteljaloux, a village in southwest France, between friendship, drunkenness and the gift of the gab, men are kings of the world. But when Jeannot gets out of jail, he has only one idea in mind: to win back Chantal, the love of his life, who has moved in with the village butcher while Jeannot was behind bars. Greek tragedy then begins to resemble a western.
