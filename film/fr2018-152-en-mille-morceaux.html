Synopsis (original)

1977, Eric Gaubert assassine Olivier, l’enfant de Nicole Parmentier. Vingt-cinq ans plus tard, cette mère à la vie brisée donne rendez-vous au meurtrier de son fils qui vient de sortir de prison. Quel est le but de cette rencontre ? Une simple vengeance ou la volonté de comprendre ce qui a poussé cet homme à commettre l’irréparable ?

Synopsis

Twenty-five years after the assassination of her son Olivier in 1977, Nicole Parmentier decides to meet with Eric Gaubert who has just been released from jail after having served his time for these crime. An explosive encounter behind closed doors between two damaged and fragile protagonists that destiny has forced together.
