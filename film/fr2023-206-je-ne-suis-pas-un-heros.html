Synopsis (original)

Louis c'est ce mec super gentil. Et dans son cabinet d'avocat, ce n'est pas un compliment. Le jour où son médecin lui diagnostique par erreur une maladie grave, le regard des autres change : on fait attention à lui, on lui pose des questions et on écoute les réponses, Louis existe enfin ! Alors bien évidemment, il hésite à dire qu'il va très bien.

Synopsis

Louis is so nice that nobody notices him. He is, in fact, missing out on his own life. The day he believes he has cancer, people around him seem to care about him for the first time, and personal and professional opportunities come pouring in. His law firm requires him to defend a multinational company accused of causing cancer by a group of affected patients. Louis discovers that existence has a price, the price of lying.
