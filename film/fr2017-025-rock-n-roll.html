Synopsis (original)

À 43 ans, Guillaume Canet a tout pour être heureux. Pourtant, sa vie de famille avec Marion, son fils, sa maison de campagne, ses chevaux lui donnent une image ringarde et plus vraiment sexy... Guillaume a compris qu'il y a urgence à tout changer. Et, pour y parvenir il va aller loin, très loin, sous le regard médusé et impuissant de son entourage.

Synopsis

At the age of 43, Guillaume Canet has every reason to be happy. Yet his life with Marion, his son, his country house, and his horses all make him look like a has-been. He seems to have lost his sex appeal. Guillaume understands that he must change everything, and fast. And he'll have to go to great lengths to do so, while his nearest and dearest look on with amazement.
