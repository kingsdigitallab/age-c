Synopsis (original)

Cuenta la historia de Antonia e Isabel que reciben el día de la muerte de su madre una caja cerrada que la difunta había guardado durante años y cuya existencia desconocían.

Synopsis

A very interesting mix of theatre and cinema, based on the play of the same title, in which from a risky and original formal proposal, the play's creative process and itself is shown, which speaks in the present time of the history of Antonia and Isabel, after their mother's dead, know the existence of a small metallic box that will lead them to discover aspects of their life never imagined, traveling to the old Yugoslavia.
