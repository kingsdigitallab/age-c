Synopsis (original)

En 1993, Max a 13 ans quand on lui offre sa première caméra. Pendant 25 ans il ne s’arrêtera pas de filmer. La bande de potes, les amours, les succès, les échecs. Des années 90 aux années 2010, c’est le portrait de toute une génération qui se dessine à travers son objectif.

Synopsis

In 1993, Max was thirteen years old when he was offered his first camera. He never stopped filming over a twenty-five-year period. His gang of friends, his loves, successes, and failures. From the 1990s through to 2010, it's the portrait of a whole generation that took shape through his lens.
