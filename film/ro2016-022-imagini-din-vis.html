Synopsis (original)

Era anul 1978 şi România se afla sub controlul dictatorului Ceauşescu. Artiştii erau persecutaţi şi închişi din cauza originii sau a opiniilor lor "inacceptabile". Printre ei, extrem de talentatul muzician Rodion Roşca. Deşi era un star radio, albumul său de debut nu a fost niciodată lansat.

Synopsis

It was 1978 and the Romanian dictator Ceausescu was in control of the country. Artists were being prosecuted and isolated due to their "unacceptable" background and views. Amongst them stood the extremely talented musician Rodion Rosca. Although he was a radio star, his debut album was never released.
