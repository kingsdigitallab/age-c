Synopsis (original)

Joaquim est musicien, mais cela ne lui permet pas de gagner sa vie. Il a un autre talent qui lui offre une opportunité de changer son existence : travailler avec son frère et se retrouver dans le cercle très fermé des traders. Pour fêter cette nouvelle destinée, il sort... et rencontre Zoé, une jeune violoniste. Ils vont passer le reste de la nuit ensemble, à parler et à chanter. Il se retrouve devant un choix : assurer son avenir ou vivre sa passion et cet amour naissant. Mais tout ceci est-il bien réel ?

Synopsis

Joaquim is a musician, but it doesn't enable him to earn a living. He has another talent, which offers him an opportunity to change his existence: working with his brother in the very closed circle of traders. To celebrate this new path, he goes out... and encounters Zoé, a young violinist. The spend the rest of the night together, talking and singing. He finds himself obliged to make a choice: ensure his future, or live his passion and this blossoming love. But is any of this real?
