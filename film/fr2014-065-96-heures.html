Synopsis (original)

Carré est le patron de la BRB (Brigade de Répression du Banditisme). 3 ans plus tôt, il a fait tomber un grand truand, Kancel. Aujourd’hui, à la faveur d’une extraction, Kancel kidnappe le flic. Il a 96 heures pour lui soutirer une seule information : savoir qui l’a balancé.

Synopsis

Carré is the boss of the BRB, the elite, special police unit of the French Ministry of the Interior fighting organized crime. 3 years earlier, he'd brought down Kancel, a major criminal. Now, during a transfer, Kancel kidnaps the cop. He has 96 hours to extract one piece of information from Carré: who snitched on him.
