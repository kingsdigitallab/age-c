Synopsis (original)

Il est 5 heures du matin, à une terrasse de café, Léon s’assoit à la table d’Eva et lui demande un chocolat chaud. Il a 11 ans et ne connaît pas ses parents. Elle a 35 ans et pas d’enfant. Elle est libre, fantasque et mène une vie de privilégiée. Il est malin, sage et vit dans un foyer. Ils ne vont plus se quitter.

Synopsis

It's five a.m. Leon sits down beside Eva at a table at a café terrace and asks her for a hot chocolate. He's eleven years old and doesn't know his parents. She is thirty-five and doesn't have any children. She is free, whimsical, and leads a privileged existence. He is smart, wise, and lives in a children's home. They'll never leave each other's side again.
